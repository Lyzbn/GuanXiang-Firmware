Two presses. One line.

Press once to spin, then again to toss. The three coins settle independently, and six lines form from the bottom upward.

02 / READING

Three ways to read.

All 64 primary and changed hexagrams offer Summary, Insight and Classical modes. Summary, with two four-character lines per hexagram, is the default; Insight covers the situation, principles, practical considerations and common misreadings; Classical presents the complete judgment. All 384 lines and both special Use texts have explanations and reflections. Long text is paginated by section, with a 16px body and at most four lines per screen.

Canonical text, excerpts from Wang Bi’s commentary and Kong Yingda’s subcommentary, and GuanXiang’s modern interpretations are labelled separately. Modern text is prepared by the project with AI assistance and checked against its sources; it is not an existing scholarly translation. Modern books are further-reading recommendations, not permissions or endorsements. Classical coverage includes 64 judgments and Great Images, 384 line texts and Small Images, and the special texts and Small Images for Qian and Kun; it is not the complete Yi Zhuan.

3Reading modes

Save your preference

Press T in settings to cycle and save the default reading mode. Relaxed or snappy animation, reduced motion and sound remain independent controls.

Try another reading

On a hexagram reading page, T changes only the current session and returns to page one. Use Up/Down or Enter to turn pages; modern modes lead to the complete classical text.

Read each line in sequence

Enter finishes each card, then advances through line text, Small Image, Insight and the next moving line, ending at the changed hexagram. All-moving Qian and Kun complete their special Use text first. History reading creates no new entry.

03 / SELF-STUDY

Eight trigrams. Sixty-four hexagrams.

Press L on the home screen to study offline. No cast is required and no history entry is created.

01

Twelve foundation lessons

Start with yin, yang and reading lines from bottom to top. Traditional mnemonics and diagrams introduce the eight trigrams, with sources distinguished from project explanations.

02

Combine two trigrams

Up/Down selects the upper or lower trigram; Left/Right changes it. See the six-line figure, name and number together. Li below Kan is Jiji; reversing them gives Weiji.

03

Explore all sixty-four

Browse the conventional order; Left/Right jumps eight entries. All 64 cards show a 40px name beside the figure, trigram labels above and two summary lines below. Continue into classical text, Insight, lines and sources.
