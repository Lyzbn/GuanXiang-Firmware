THREE COINS · SIX LINES
01 / CASTING

Two presses. One line.

Press once to spin, then again to toss. The three coins settle independently, and six lines form from the bottom upward.

01

Spin

Press Enter to start the three coins spinning.

02

Toss

Press Enter again and let each coin settle.

03

Read

Complete all six lines, then explore the primary hexagram, moving lines and changed hexagram.

02 / READING

Three ways to read.

All 64 primary and changed hexagrams offer Summary, Insight and Classical modes. Summary, with two four-character lines per hexagram, is the default; Insight adds a longer modern explanation, and Classical presents the complete judgment. Long texts are fully paginated.

Modern readings are project interpretations of classical commentaries, not an existing scholarly translation. The classical collection contains 64 complete judgments, 384 line texts and their 384 Small Image commentaries, plus the applicable special texts and commentaries for Qian and Kun. This is not the complete Yi Zhuan collection.

3Reading modes

Save your preference

Press T in settings to cycle and save the default reading mode. Relaxed or snappy animation, reduced motion and sound remain independent controls.

Try another reading

On a hexagram reading page, T changes only the current session and returns to page one. Use Up/Down or Enter to turn pages; modern modes lead to the complete classical text.

Revisit your history

Opening an entry applies the saved default without creating another history entry. Moving lines retain their complete line texts and Small Image commentaries.

03 / INSTALLATION

Choose your installation path.

For M5Stack Cardputer ADV only. Check the package instructions, checksums and partition layout before flashing.

FIRST INSTALL / SWITCHING FIRMWARE

Complete merged image

Write at 0x0 to install the bootloader, partition table and application. This resets history and settings; back up first.

Your first reading

  1. Press Enter on the home screen; recording an intention is optional.
  2. Press Enter twice for each of the six lines.
  3. Follow the footer controls to read the hexagrams and moving lines.
  4. Use H for history and I for settings; T in settings saves the default reading mode.
  5. On a hexagram reading page, T switches temporarily; Up/Down or Enter turns pages.
